## What air pressure tells you about a storm
Air pressure drops as a storm gets closer. On a normal Florida day, sea-level pressure is usually around 1012 to 1018 hPa and drifts up and down by 1 to 2 hPa through the day. A tropical storm nearby often pushes it below about 1005 hPa, and a hurricane close by can push it much lower, sometimes dropping several hPa per hour near the center. When pressure stops falling and starts rising steadily, the storm center is usually moving away. StormBox watches these changes with its own barometer, so it works without internet.
Source: StormBox team

## What the storm phases mean
StormBox shows one of these phases based on pressure history. "Falling" means pressure is dropping and the storm may be getting closer. "Possible eye" means pressure stopped falling near its lowest point after a fast drop; the eye's calm is temporary and the wind will return from the other direction, so stay inside. "Recovering" means pressure is rising and the worst may be passing, but stay inside until officials say it is safe.
Source: StormBox team; NWS - Hurricane Safety <https://www.weather.gov/media/safety/hurricane_safety.pdf>

## What the storm risk score means
The storm risk score is an early warning from a small decision tree trained on real NOAA records of Florida pressure and past hurricanes. It estimates how likely it is that a tropical storm or hurricane center comes within about 150 km in the next 12 hours. It is a score, not an exact chance, and it never sets off alarms by itself. Official forecasts from the National Hurricane Center and local officials always come first.
Source: StormBox team

## What the StormBox alarms mean
Alarms come from fixed rules in the box's safety chip, not from AI, and keep working even if the assistant is off. Flood: water detected by the floor sensor. Air: the air sensor detects fumes or smoke; it is not a certified carbon monoxide detector. Heat: the room has been dangerously hot for a while. Storm: pressure dropped fast over 3 hours. A long press on the button silences an alarm. A message marked "Replay" is recorded NOAA data being played back for a demonstration, not a live storm.
Source: StormBox team
